Export Process

What to expect from first inquiry to delivery at your port.

Timeline

  • Inquiry & Needs Assessment

    You submit an inquiry with product, quantity, and destination. We confirm feasibility and timing.

  • Quotation

    We send a formal quote covering price, packaging, and delivery terms for your review.

  • Agreement & Order Confirmation

    Once terms are agreed, we confirm the order and payment schedule.

  • Sourcing / Production & Quality Check

    Produce is sourced, sorted, and inspected against agreed specifications.

  • Packaging & Loading

    Goods are packaged for export and loaded for shipment.

  • Shipping & Logistics

    We coordinate freight and track the shipment to its destination port.

  • Delivery & Documentation Handover

    Shipping documents are handed over so you can clear customs on arrival.
